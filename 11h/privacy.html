11H

Privacy Policy

Updated November 14, 2026

The short version

Your birth date, birth time and birth place never leave your phone. 11H computes your chart on the device. If you create an account, the only astrological data stored on our servers is what you choose to publish: the sign and degree of your planets and houses, which cannot be turned back into a date, a time or a place.

What stays on your phone

Birth details for you and the people you add, your course progress, your settings and your subscription status are kept in the app’s own storage on your device. They are included in your normal device backup if you have one. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we store if you join

You can use the chart, the readings, the forecast and the course without an account. If you sign in with Apple, we store: a random account ID, the email Apple gives us (which may be Apple’s private relay address), your username, display name, bio and avatar, your published positions (sign and degree of each planet and house, if you choose to publish them), the people you follow or block, your posts, likes and comments, and the date you accepted the house rules.

Direct messages you send are stored on our servers so they can be delivered and shown to both people in the conversation. They are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read them, except a message that has been reported, which a person reviews to decide whether a rule was broken. Deleting a message removes it from our servers; deleting your account removes all of them.

Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method. We never see your Apple password.

What other members can see

Your username, display name, avatar, bio and your Sun, Moon and Rising signs are visible to other members when your profile is public. Your published positions are visible only if you chose to publish them. Set your profile to private and only the people you approve can see it. Nobody can see your birth date, time or place because we do not have them.

Subscriptions

11H Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment and keeps your payment details; we receive only a receipt that says whether a subscription is active and when it renews. We do not see your card number.

What we do not do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or sharing of personal data with data brokers, no analytics SDKs from third parties. We do not read your location except to turn a birth place you type into coordinates, and that lookup result stays on your phone.

Service providers

Account data is hosted by Supabase (database and file storage) on servers in the United States. Sign-in is handled by Apple. These providers process data only to run 11H for us.

Children

11H is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Your choices

Delete your account at any time from Profile > Account > Delete account. This removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows and avatar from our servers immediately. You can also unpublish your positions, make your profile private, or block anyone, all from the app.

To ask what we hold about you, or for anything else, email support@maplewirelabs.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you the next time you open it. The date at the top is the date of the current version.
