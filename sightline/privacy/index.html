Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Sightline is made by Maplewire Labs (Abunasr Zhanybekov, Ontario, Canada). This policy explains what the Sightline app does with your information.

The short version: Sightline does not collect any personal data. Everything the app measures, saves and records stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Sightline has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking and it contains no third-party code that collects data. We never see your location, photos, videos or readings.

Permissions and how they are used

Sightline asks for these permissions only to make its features work. Everything happens on your iPhone:

  • Camera: shows the view you are aiming at and takes your photos and videos.
  • Microphone: records sound with your videos, only while you are recording. You can turn video sound off in the Video settings.
  • Location (only while you use the app): corrects the compass to true north and adds your coordinates and altitude to photos and to the reading log.
  • Motion sensors: measure where the camera is pointing and how the phone is tilted.
  • Photos (add only): saves your photos and videos to your photo library. Sightline cannot see your other photos.

Information stored on your iPhone

Your settings and the reading log are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are deleted when you delete Sightline. A CSV file of the log is created only when you choose to export it and it goes only where you send it.

Photos and videos you share

Photos taken with Sightline can show the direction, angle, date, time and location on the picture and the location and direction are also saved inside the photo file. If you share a photo, the people you share it with can see this information.

Purchases

Sightline Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only learns whether Sightline Pro has been bought on your Apple ID.

Children

Sightline is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@maplewirelabs.com.
