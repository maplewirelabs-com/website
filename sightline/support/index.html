Common questions

The compass reading looks wrong. Move away from metal and magnets (cars, railings, phone cases with magnets), then calibrate: Tools, then Calibrate compass and move the phone in a figure 8.

True north is not available. Allow location access for Sightline in the iPhone Settings app. Sightline uses your location only to correct the compass to true north and to stamp photos.

I bought Sightline Pro on another iPhone. Open Tools, then About Sightline and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I have an offer code. Open Tools, then About Sightline and tap Redeem Code.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
