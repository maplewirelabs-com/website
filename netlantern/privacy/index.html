Netlantern Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version: Netlantern has no account, no advertising and no tracking. What the app measures stays on your iPhone. If you buy Netlantern Pro, the purchase is recorded without your name.

What we collect

Nothing about you or your network. Netlantern does not send what it measures to us. We have no servers that receive it and the app contains no analytics and no advertising code. The only record kept outside your iPhone is whether Netlantern Pro was bought, described under Purchases.

Wi-Fi network name and location

  • Netlantern shows the name of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to and uses it to label your saved tests.
  • iOS only lets an app see the network name after you allow location access, so the app asks for that permission. This is Apple's rule, because a network name can hint at where you are.
  • Netlantern does not read your position. It never asks iOS for coordinates and it does not store or send your location.
  • The Home screen can show an approximate location (a city or region). It is worked out from your public internet address by the speed test server, not by your iPhone's location services. It is shown on screen only.
  • You can refuse or remove the location permission at any time in the iPhone's Settings. The app keeps working; it just cannot show the network name.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Speed test results, signal and mobile data readings, room and place names you type, your own names for devices and the list of devices already seen on your network.
  • Wi-Fi passwords you save for making QR codes. These are kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

None of this leaves your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

  • Netlantern Pro is optional. It is sold as a yearly subscription or as a one-time purchase and Apple takes the payment. We never see your name, your card or your Apple Account.
  • To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. It receives a random ID made for this copy of the app, what was bought and when and Apple's receipt for it. It does not receive your name, your email or anything the app measures.
  • You can ask for that purchase record to be deleted by writing to the address below.

Other permissions

  • Local Network: used to find the devices on your own Wi-Fi network and to check that your router answers. The results stay on your iPhone.
  • Camera: used only to read Wi-Fi QR codes. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Shortcuts: if you set it up, the app asks Apple's Shortcuts app for signal readings. They stay on your iPhone.

Services the app contacts

Some tools need to reach a public internet service to do their job. As with any internet connection, that service sees your public internet address and whatever you asked it to look up. No name, account or device identifier is sent.

  • Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com, cloudflare-dns.com, 1.1.1.1): speed tests, your public address and provider, connection checks and name lookups.
  • Google (dns.google, www.gstatic.com): name lookups and connection checks.
  • rdap.org and the official internet registries it forwards to: Whois records for an address or site you look up.
  • Team Cymru: its public address-to-network map tells traceroute who runs each hop. It is asked through the name lookup services above.
  • Apple (captive.apple.com): checks whether a Wi-Fi sign-in page is in the way.
  • Expo: the app checks here for updates to itself.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: prices, purchases and restoring a purchase, as described under Purchases.
  • Sites and addresses you choose to ping, trace or scan.

Each of these services handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Children

Netlantern is a general tool and is not aimed at children. It collects nothing from anyone.
