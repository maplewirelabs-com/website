Common questions

Why is the speed different from my car's? Car speedometers usually read a little high on purpose. GPS speed is often closer to your real speed, but it can be off for a moment in tunnels, between tall buildings or right after you start.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone? Open Settings › About and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I use a code? Tap Redeem Code on the Pro screen or in Settings › About.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
